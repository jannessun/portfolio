01

Brand Foundations & Consistency Framework

Seoul InkBrand CommunicationsSeoul, Korea
Context

Seoul Ink is a creative studio operating across Korean and international markets. At the time, the brand lacked cohesive visual and messaging standards across channels.

Approach
  • Defined a clear brand voice adaptable across cultures.
  • Prioritized consistency over trend-driven visuals.
  • Established scalable guidelines rather than fixed executions.
  • Ensured brand elements could be applied across digital and physical touchpoints.
Outcome
  • Led brand communication strategy and advised on typography and direction.
  • Developed a foundational brand book with consistency principles across visual and messaging systems.
  • A framework built to scale across cultures and touchpoints, not a fixed set of assets.

Reconstructed case study based on original brand direction work.

02

European Chocolate, A Study in Quiet Craft

Concept StudyBrand DirectionItaly
Context

A concept exploring European chocolate through the lens of quiet craftsmanship and nostalgia, prioritizing warmth, restraint, and emotional comfort while remaining commercially grounded. Designed to feel familiar, human, and enduring rather than performative.

Direction
  • Feeling: quiet, nostalgic, warm, craft-led, enduring, consumer-aware. Designed to comfort, not impress.
  • Typography: restrained editorial type selected for warmth, clarity, and longevity; legibility and quiet confidence over display.
  • Colour: warm neutrals with subtle contrast, to evoke comfort, familiarity, and timelessness.
Outcome
  • A coherent brand direction (feeling, type, and colour) grounded enough to carry into commercial product.

Conceptual exploration informed by chocolate studies in Italy.
